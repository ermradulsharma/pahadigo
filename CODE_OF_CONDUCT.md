# 🏔️ PahadiGo Code of Conduct

## Our Inclusive Pledge

At PahadiGo, we believe that open-source communities representing the majestic Himalayan region should mirror its diversity, resilience, and welcoming spirit.

In the interest of fostering an open, highly collaborative, and deeply respectful environment, we pledge to prioritize participation in our project and our community to be a harassment-free experience for absolutely everyone—regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 🤝 Our Core Standards

Examples of community behavior that directly contribute to creating a positive environment include:

* **Leading with Empathy**: Operating with kindness and seeking to understand alternate engineering perspectives.
* **Graceful Inclusion**: Proactively utilizing welcoming, neutral, and inclusive language within all PRs, commits, and discussions.
* **Respectful Disagreement**: Demonstrating utmost respect for differing viewpoints, regardless of a contributor’s hierarchy.
* **Constructive Feedback**: Submitting and receiving technical or architectural feedback graciously.
* **Community Focus**: Orienting decisions towards what is objectively best for the overall PahadiGo travel ecosystem.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention/advances.
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and inflammatory political or personal attacks.
* Public or private harassment of community members.
* Publishing others' explicit private information (like a physical or electronic address) without stringent explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate within a highly professional workplace setting.

## ⚖️ Enforcement and Governance

Project maintainers bear the explicit responsibility for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior. Maintaining these exact standards is critical. Maintainers have the right and explicit responsibility to surgically remove, actively edit, or unconditionally reject PRs, commits, code, wiki edits, tracker issues, and other contributions that drastically misalign with this Code of Conduct.

Project maintainers also hold the right to temporarily ban or permanently expel any contributor for behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or actively harmful.

## 🚨 Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or strictly unacceptable behavior may be reported rapidly by contacting the core project leadership directly at `conduct@pahadigo.com`.

All formal complaints will be swiftly reviewed and vigorously investigated. Rest assured, the review team respects strict privacy and security configurations for the reporter of any incident.
