## 🚨 2. Asynchronous Vulnerability Dispatch Center

**All suspected penetrations or anomalies MUST be emailed securely to security@pahadigo.com.**

### Isolation Parameters Evaluated

Any reports detailing logic bypass functions, JWT forgery routines, or NoSQL Object injection bypasses will be rewarded and patched via our strict SLA:

- **Triage**: 24-48 Hours
- **Mitigation Deployment**: Within 5 Business Days

For more details on explicit cryptography implementations, refer to `AuthService.js`.

---

## 🚫 3. Out of Scope Vulnerabilities

To ensure our security team focuses on high-impact threats, the following are **strictly out of scope** and will not be eligible for reward or review:
- Volumetric/Network-level Denial of Service (DDoS/DoS) attacks.
- Social Engineering, Phishing, or physical attacks against employees/users.
- Clickjacking on non-sensitive, public-facing marketing pages.
- Self-XSS (User tricking themselves into executing code).
- Issues related to SPF/DKIM/DMARC configurations without a proven exploit chain.

## 💰 4. Bug Bounty & Recognition Program

We value the ethical hacker community. For valid, in-scope security vulnerabilities that have a demonstrably critical impact on the PahadiGo ecosystem, we offer:
- **Monetary Bounties**: Awarded at the discretion of the security team based on the CVSS score.
- **Hall of Fame**: Public recognition for researchers on our contributors page.
- **PahadiGo Swag**: Sent to researchers providing exceptionally well-documented reports.

## ⚖️ 5. Safe Harbor Policy

If you conduct your security research in good faith and comply with this policy, we consider your research to be authorized. **PahadiGo will not initiate or support any legal action** related to your research. We will work with you to understand and resolve the issue quickly.

---

## ⏳ 6. Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We require strict confidentiality during the patching process. 
- You must allow us a minimum of **90 days** from the date the patch is deployed before publicly disclosing the vulnerability (e.g., on a blog, GitHub, or Twitter).
- Please request explicit authorization from `security@pahadigo.com` before publishing your findings.

---

## 🔒 7. PGP Encryption

For highly sensitive reports involving zero-day exploits or active data leaks, please encrypt your email using our public PGP Key.
*(A public key will be provided upon initial unencrypted contact or via our Keybase profile).*

## 💳 8. Compliance & Automated Defense

PahadiGo is architected with modern defense-in-depth methodologies:
- **Automated Scanning**: We enforce strict CI/CD security pipelines. All pull requests are automatically scanned for vulnerable dependencies (via tools like Dependabot) and patched immediately.
- **Financial Compliance**: We do not store raw card data. All financial transactions are tokenized and processed via **PCI-DSS compliant** payment gateways (Razorpay).
